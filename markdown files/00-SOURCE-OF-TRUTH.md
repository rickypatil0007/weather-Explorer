
## Apple-Inspired Enhancements (Added Phase 1)
1. **Dynamic Full-Page Weather Background:** A global background engine that renders clear, cloudy, rain, snow, or starry night CSS scenes based on the active location's weather and local time.
2. **Reverse Geocoding:** Clicking anywhere on the map triggers an OSM Nominatim reverse-geocode to resolve the real city/country name.
3. **Advanced Forecasts:** Addition of a 24-hour horizontal scrolling forecast (with Condition, Precip, Wind modes) and expanding the daily forecast to 10 days where supported.
4. **Air Quality & UV:** Integration of Open-Meteo Air Quality API (AQI, PM2.5, PM10) and UV Index.
5. **Weather Map Modes:** Leaflet map enhancements to support Temperature, Precipitation, Wind, and AQI overlay layers.
6. **Location Management:** localStorage-based saved locations and recent searches.
